Frequently asked questions

Is there a service fee?

No. We do not add a service fee. The total shown includes cleaning and an estimate of taxes; the final price is confirmed when you reserve.

How do I book a viewing?

Pick a property, choose a day and time, and send your details. Viewings can be in person or by video call.
